From First Look To Your Own Gate

A clear five-step path. Most buyers go from first call to registration in 3–6 weeks.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Browse listings, watch aerial tours and shortlist properties. Tell us your use — farming, homestead, retreat or long-term holding.

  2. 02

    Visit the land

    We schedule a guided site visit. Walk the boundaries, meet neighbours, see water sources and road access in person.

  3. 03

    Verify everything

    Receive the title report, survey map and utility documents. Have your own lawyer review them — we encourage it.

  4. 04

    Agree & register

    Sign a clear sale agreement with an itemised cost sheet, then complete registration at the local land office.

  5. 05

    Take the keys

    Physical handover with boundary walk-through. Optional land care services to fence, plant or lease your acres.

Can I visit a property before committing?

Always. We strongly recommend at least one guided visit. Book a slot through the form below and we'll coordinate timing, directions and a local expert to meet you on site.

What documents will I receive?

A title report, survey/plat map with GPS corners, tax records, any easements or encumbrances, and utility documentation (water, power, road access). You're encouraged to have your own lawyer review everything.

Do you help with financing?

We can introduce you to lenders who specialise in land and agricultural loans. Rates and approval are set by the lender.

Can someone manage the land after I buy?

Yes. Our optional land-care service covers fencing, plantation, caretaking and finding vetted tenants if you'd like to lease the land to local farmers.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Before you sign, you get an itemised sheet showing the property price, registration charges, taxes and any service fees. What's on the sheet is what you pay.
